Zen Tabs Cleaner — Settings

Inactivity threshold

An unpinned tab is closed if it hasn't been visited for this long. Active tabs, pinned tabs, Essentials and tabs playing audio are never closed.

hours

Check frequency

How often the extension looks for inactive tabs. A shorter interval closes overdue tabs sooner but wakes the extension more often — 15 minutes is plenty for thresholds of an hour or more.

minutes

Excluded domains

One domain per line (e.g. gmail.com). Tabs on these domains and their subdomains are never closed.

Notifications

Show a notification after each cleanup — click it to restore the closed tabs. The last cleanup can always be restored from the popup.
